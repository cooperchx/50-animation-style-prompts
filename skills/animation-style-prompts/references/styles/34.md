## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Bold graphic 2D action-animation style: extreme angular stylisation, characters built from sharp wedges and long tapering limbs, huge wedge-shaped torsos on thin legs, no outlines, flat colour with a single hard-edged shadow tone, minimal facial detail with narrow eyes and big brows.
Palette: #4F7A76, #D9C7A3, #B23A2E, #B86A44, #1E2230.
Even graphic light, hard flat shadows, dramatic foreground framing, broad cinematic staging and deliberate pause/burst timing.
Negative: 3D render, soft painterly gradients, realistic anatomy, anime faces, photoreal.
```

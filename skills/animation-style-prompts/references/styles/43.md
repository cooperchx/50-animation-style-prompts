## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Stylized 3D animated feature-film look, high-quality CG with subsurface-scattered skin, oversized expressive eyes, big curly hair, exaggerated squash-and-stretch cartoon proportions, freckles and fabric micro-detail.
Palette: #E0A45A, #2F6B73, #A23B2A.
Soft global illumination, warm window-like key light, shallow depth of field, expressive squash-and-stretch and playful heartfelt timing.
Negative: 2D, painterly, dark horror lighting, realistic humans, low-poly.
```

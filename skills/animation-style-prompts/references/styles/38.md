## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Minimalist stylized 3D storybook: hand-carved weathered stone or unglazed-clay toy surfaces with chipped edges, hairline cracks and matte porosity; simple geometric bodies, thin stick limbs, glossy cartoon eyes and exaggerated emotional mouths.
Palette: #B9B29E, #8C8878, #6F6B4F, #D8D6CC.
Desaturated diffuse overcast light, soft atmospheric fog, low camera angles, restrained slow movement and quiet fable mood.
Negative: vivid colors, glossy plastic, busy backgrounds, realistic animals.
```

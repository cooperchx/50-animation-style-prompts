## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
1990s Japanese anime film look, hand-painted cel animation with clean dark ink lines, flat cel shading with soft airbrushed gradients, slightly soft film-transfer grain and gentle chromatic bleed.
Palette: #5B5A86, #E9A27A, #C9A15A, #6B6E78, #F6E7C2.
Twilight warm/cool contrast, soft glowing highlights, long shadows, low-angle tracking and analog cinematic motion.
Negative: 3D render, modern digital anime gloss, chibi, photoreal, oversaturated neon.
```

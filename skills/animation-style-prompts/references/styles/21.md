## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Painterly low-poly comic art: characters and environments built from large flat faceted polygons, each facet hand-painted with subtle brush texture, no outlines, chunky angular graphic-novel anatomy with blocky jaws and big forearms.
Palette: #F07A1E, #F6B23A, #1E2533, #6E6B5A, #C9C98A.
Hard cinematic light, strong polygonal shadow planes, dramatic warm/cool contrast, forceful framing and graphic-novel action energy.
Negative: smooth realistic CG, anime, soft watercolour, cute chibi.
```

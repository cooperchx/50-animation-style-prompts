## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Retro editorial cartoon / mid-century storybook animation: flat graphic shapes with a single dominant colour wash, dry-brush and grainy print texture over every surface, simplified characters with long legs, tiny feet, small heads and minimal faces, very few lines.
Palette: #1E5A6E, #0E2A35, #6FB1C4, #E3874A, #EDEDE6.
Restricted monochromatic wash, minimal shadows, strong one-point perspective, graphic symmetry and understated comic timing.
Negative: 3D render, photoreal, full rainbow palette, anime, glossy cel shading.
```

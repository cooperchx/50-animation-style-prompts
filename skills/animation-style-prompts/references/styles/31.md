## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Romantic digital oil painting, visible impasto brush strokes and palette-knife texture, loose impressionist edges, painterly but readable figures.
Palette: #E9A46B, #5C86C4, #F2B24A, #6D7580.
Golden-hour and twilight glow, luminous painted reflections, graceful flowing motion, soft transitions and romantic atmosphere.
Negative: anime, flat vector, 3D render, harsh outlines.
```

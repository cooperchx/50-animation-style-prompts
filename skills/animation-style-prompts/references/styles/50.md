## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Handmade claymation stop-motion look, plasticine characters with visible fingerprints, tool marks and slightly lumpy hand-sculpted surfaces, matte clay with soft specular sheen.
Palette: #3B67A8, #E07A2F, #E3B64A, #8D8F94, #CDB48C.
Soft studio key light, miniature tabletop depth of field, tactile hand-sculpted volume, gently stepped 12-fps-like motion and awkward playful timing.
Negative: smooth CG plastic, 2D drawing, photoreal creatures, dark grim lighting.
```

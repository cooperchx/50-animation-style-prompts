## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Architectural expressionist visual design, stylized cinematic 3D with a painted matte-painting finish: colossal tilted geometric masses, impossible vertical layering, leaning forms, sharp distorted perspectives, patched weathered surfaces and faded color blocks like worn collage; extreme scale contrast.
Palette: #8A8A86, #A84536, #4A5C78, #DCD3C2, #C9C7C2.
Hazy diffuse light, extreme angular perspective, immense depth and scale contrast, vertiginous framing and cinematic motion.
Negative: bright cartoon colours, flat 2D, clean modern glass architecture, anime, night neon.
```

## 美学提示词（中文）

```text
建筑表现主义视觉设计：风格化电影 3D 结合手绘接景质感；巨大倾斜几何体块、不可能的垂直层叠、歪斜形体、尖锐扭曲透视、斑驳风化表面与旧拼贴般褪色色块，强调极端尺度对比。
色板：#8A8A86、#A84536、#4A5C78、#DCD3C2、#C9C7C2。
雾化漫射光、极端棱角透视、巨大纵深与尺度对比、眩晕构图与电影运镜。
负面词：明亮卡通色、扁平 2D、干净的现代玻璃建筑、日漫、夜间霓虹。
```

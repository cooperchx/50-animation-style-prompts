## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Kids' doodle picture-book style: wobbly imperfect hand-drawn black marker outlines, crayon and coloured-pencil fills with visible strokes, simple goofy shapes, bulging googly eyes and huge open-mouthed grins, deliberately naive proportions.
Palette: #F2B630, #7C8F3E, #6E8094, #D45A36, #E4C98A.
Even picture-book light, simple layered compositions, naive hand-drawn perspective, elastic cartoon movement and cheerful playfulness.
Negative: realistic animals, 3D render, polished vector, dark moody lighting.
```

## 美学提示词（中文）

```text
儿童涂鸦绘本风：歪歪扭扭、不完美的手绘黑色马克笔轮廓，蜡笔和彩铅填色、可见笔触，造型简单傻气，鼓出的大眼和咧开的大嘴，故意稚拙的比例。
色板：#F2B630、#7C8F3E、#6E8094、#D45A36、#E4C98A。
均匀绘本平光、简单分层构图、稚拙手绘透视、弹性卡通动作与欢乐俏皮气质。
负面词：写实动物、3D 渲染、精致矢量、阴暗情绪光。
```

```text
Stylised sci-fi concept art mixing graphic-novel character design, anime faces and Western comic rendering with a dieselpunk-cyberpunk world: clean confident linework, soft cel shading with painted gradients, detailed mechanical gear.
Palette: #4E6177, #8A5A3A, #CDB898, #49C9E6, #E39A3A.
Dusty atmospheric light and volumetric shafts, warm/cool contrast, layered cinematic depth and detailed yet readable design.
Negative: photoreal, chibi, flat vector, bright pastel kawaii, low detail.
```

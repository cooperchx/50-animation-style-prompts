## 美学提示词（英文）

```text
Interlocking studded toy-brick aesthetic: all forms assembled from visible modular plastic bricks; cylindrical heads, claw-like hands and rigid simplified toy proportions. Glossy injection-moulded ABS plastic, specular highlights, subtle mould seams, practical miniature photography, macro optics, shallow depth of field and tilt-shift bokeh.
Palette: #F7C600, #1E8E3E, #1F3C88, #D52B1E.
Practical miniature lighting, bright primary colors, macro focus falloff, rigid toy articulation and gently stepped stop-motion cadence.
Negative: soft clay, cartoon 2D, realistic human skin, painterly texture.
```
